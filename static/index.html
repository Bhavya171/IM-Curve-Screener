Dec–Dec spreads & flies

Each Dec–Dec block lists every structure starting in that year (Dec, Mar, Jun, Sep). Spread = front − back. 3MF = front − 2×middle + back on legs 3M apart (DMJ, MJS, JSD, SDM); 6MF the same on legs 6M apart (DJD, MSM, JDJ, SMS); 6MF − 2×3MF uses the 3MF with the same front leg (DJD − 2DMJ, MSM − 2MJS, JDJ − 2JSD, SMS − 2SDM). All in bp. Hover a cell to see each leg's price. Shading is the change since the previous settlement (green = up, red = down). Italic grey = a leg has no usable price on the chosen basis (no two-sided quote for Mid; for Last, its last trade is over 30 minutes older than the freshest leg). Click any cell to chart that structure's curve for every market.
