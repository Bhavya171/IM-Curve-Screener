Spread = front − back; fly = front − 2×middle + back; condor = front − 2nd − 3rd + 4th; double fly = front − 3×2nd + 3×3rd − 4th (legs 3M, 6M, 9M, 1Y or 2Y apart). Combined structures add up leg by leg, e.g. 1Y fly − 2×6MF on Dec26 = −Dec26 + 4×Jun27 − 4×Dec27 + Dec28. A saved strategy is added everywhere: a column group in the Screener, its own strip in Strips, a clickable curve chart, and in the Curve simulator as inputs you can type, a chart and a table. Saved strategies can also be used as building blocks. They're saved in this browser.

Curve simulator · inputs

live (default, follows the market) your input moved to fit your inputs Each year column holds the Dec–Dec spread and every 3M spread, 3MF and 6MF starting in it (bp). Type any box and the rest follow with the smallest change from live: a 3M spread moves just that spread (Dec–Dec becomes the new sum); a Dec–Dec is shared equally by the 3M spreads you have not fixed; a fly moves its belly (3MF +2 → front spread +1, back spread −1; 6MF +2 → front two 3M spreads +0.5 each, back two −0.5). Everything not typed stays changeable. If a box is already decided by newer inputs, the oldest one in the clash is released. Clear a box (or press Delete) to send it back to live. The small figure under each box is sim − live. Pick a market above; the charts and tables below always show every market.
